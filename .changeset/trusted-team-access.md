- **Comments from teammates with team access**: the review now reads the comments of anyone with admin or write access to the repo, as GitHub's permission check reports it, along with its owners, members and collaborators. Before, a teammate whose access came through an org team, or whose org membership is private, had their comments left out, so a reply such as "this is intentional" didn't reach the next review. The action asks once per commenter. When the check fails, that commenter's comments stay out and the job log warns.
